# Gargantua VR: Asset Credits & Licensing

## Asset Usage Policy
This project contains **100% original, procedurally generated, or MIT/Apache 2.0 open-source code and assets**. It contains no copyrighted movie footage, spacecraft models, audio files, logos, or extracted assets from any commercial films.

---

## 1. Procedural Assets
* **Accretion Disk, Noise & Geodesic Raymarching Shader**: Original HLSL/GLSL implementation by the Gargantua development team based on published General Relativity papers (Bardeen 1972, Novikov-Thorne 1973).
* **Space-Suit Body Meshes**: Generated procedurally at runtime via `ProceduralMeshes.cs`.

## 2. Open-Source References & Mathematics
* **Planck Blackbody Algorithm**: Based on public domain color temperature approximations by Tanner Helland & Mitchell Charity.
* **ACES Tone Mapping**: Public domain ACES filmic S-curve formulation by Stephen Hill.
* **Kerr Math Equations**: Standard General Relativity closed forms from Chandrasekhar (*The Mathematical Theory of Black Holes*) and Bardeen, Press & Teukolsky (1972).
